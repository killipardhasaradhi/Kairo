Character profile

Meet Kairo

The Last Dream Wanderer. A small, poised Veyling who walks through forgotten dreams and broken timelines.

Name
Kairo
Title
The Last Dream Wanderer
Species
Veyling: a being born between two moments in time
Profession
Dream Wanderer and Keeper of Lost Memories
Appearance
Flowing midnight-blue cloak, a hood with a star-shaped tip, large golden eyes, uneven translucent cyan-and-violet ears, and a brass lantern holding a clock with no hands.
Personality
  • Curious
  • Brave
  • Playful under pressure
  • Stubborn
  • Observant
  • Secretly afraid of being forgotten
Goal
Find out why tomorrow keeps resetting, and let time move forward without destroying the worlds caught in it.
Weakness
Every reset erases memories, so it is hard to tell a new discovery from one made before.
Signature lantern
A brass lantern with a handless clock. It ticks backward and shows messages from other versions of Kairo.
Shadow anomaly
Kairo's shadow sometimes moves one second before Kairo does.
Tagline
“Yesterday left me a warning. Tomorrow left me a trap.”

Explore Kairo's World

Eight short scenes. Click the glowing clues, collect them in the notebook, and decide what to do with the thirteenth bell.

Skip the story and view the portfolio at any time. You can also jump to any scene below.

How to explore (30 seconds)

  1. Go scene by scene. There are 8 scenes. Use the numbered buttons below, or the big button at the bottom of each scene to move on.
  2. Each scene says “What to do here”. Do exactly what it says. It tells you which things to tap.
  3. Gold buttons marked ★ are clues. Tap one and it is saved in your Detective Notebook. A ✓ means you already found it.
  4. See your clues any time with the round lantern button at the bottom-right, or the Notebook link in the menu.
  5. You do not have to find every clue. You can always press the next button and the story still makes sense.

        Live Kairo

        She watches your cursor (or your finger). Move around her, hold still, get close to her face, then try tapping her ears, her hood star, her lantern, her cheeks three times, or her cloak: she turns around and her expression changes.

        Her mood

        • Her eyes, head, ears and cloak each follow you differently.
        • Stay still and she wanders and blinks on her own.
        • Come very close to her face and she is startled.
        • Stay on her a moment and she smiles.
        • Tap her cloak or hands: she spins around and shows a new expression.
        • Switch Night or Twilight in the header and her mood follows.
